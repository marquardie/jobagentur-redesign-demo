Questions students ask

How much will I earn?

The usual hourly rate is €15.87. Some employers pay more or less (€14.60–19.00). Students work 172 hours a month on average, depending on the employer's needs and your situation. Most workplaces offer overtime if you want it.

What job will I do?

You're placed in the position your employer needs most, based on several criteria. The Jobs section shows which fields have openings and which are most popular.

Where in Germany will I work?

Students work in cities and towns across Germany. In 2025 most went to Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Cologne, Karlsruhe, Dresden, Düsseldorf, Leipzig, Stuttgart, Hamburg and Nuremberg.

Where will I live?

Your employer arranges housing and you sign a rental contract when you arrive. The rent is deducted from your pay, at most €20 per day. Sometimes a €200 deposit is required, usually taken from your first pay and returned when you leave. We tell you about any differences before you travel.

Can I work with my friends?

You can apply together, but we can't promise 100% that you'll work and live together.

What do you do, exactly?

Jobagentur TR is not your employer. We refer you to an employer, prepare your work permit documents, submit the permit application, help you get ready to travel and coordinate your housing. During your stay we run a 24/7 emergency phone line.

How many times can I take part?

You can take part for 90 days within 12 months. Students in their graduation year can't apply, and the age limit is 32.

Will you help me when I arrive?

Yes. We support you during orientation, from using public transport to getting a mobile number and opening a bank account.
